Meet the CEO

Every new hire gets their own welcome from the CEO. Amara, a made-up CEO, greets them by name, links their team's work to the mission, and answers their questions from your company story.

Language
Company story Tallyfield · why we started, mission, values, teams (sample)

The sample: the story of Tallyfield, a made-up company: why it started, its mission, three values, this year's goal and what each team does.

Your own story: paste it. Use headings, and a short section for each team ("### Sales"), so the CEO can link each new hire's team to the mission.

Amara is made up. She only says what the story says, and never invents numbers, plans or events.

The next welcome uses this story. It stays in this page and is not stored.
